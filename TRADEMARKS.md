# Axiom name and logo

The Apache-2.0 [license](LICENSE) covers Axiom's code. Section 6 of that
license states that it grants no rights to the project's trademarks: the
**Axiom name, the Axiom wordmark, the A-mark and the Axiom penguin mascot**
(the files in `branding/identity/` and `branding/mascots/`).

## You may

- redistribute **unmodified** official Axiom images and source with the
  branding included;
- use the name and logo to refer to the project, for example in articles,
  talks, reviews or "works with Axiom" notes;
- use the branding in community material that clearly isn't official, such as
  wallpapers, stickers or meetup slides.

## Please ask first if you want to

- ship a **modified** system or fork under the Axiom name or with the Axiom
  logo. Rebrand the fork instead; the code itself is yours to use under the
  Apache License;
- use the branding in a way that suggests official endorsement, or in a product
  name or domain name.

To ask, open an issue or contact the maintainer through
[github.com/Prof-bilal](https://github.com/Prof-bilal).

The asset provenance review for `branding/` is still a release blocker, as
recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
