# Vision

Axiom aims to make a Linux computer a coherent place to develop, understand system behavior and delegate bounded work to AI agents. Its identity comes from trustworthy workflows and consistent interaction, rather than imitation of another desktop.

The first users are developers willing to evaluate an early Arch-based prototype in a VM or spare machine. They need project startup, understandable system changes, evidence when diagnosing trouble, and recovery when an experiment fails. Mainstream replacement claims require later hardware, accessibility, support and upgrade evidence.

Borrow continuity and discoverability ideas from macOS, Windows, Android and Linux by identifying the underlying problem: resume work, find controls, understand permissions and move files safely. Do not copy protected visual assets or make macOS cloning the product requirement.

Product pillars: local control; cloud-optional core operation; visible delegated authority; a consistent Qt/Plasma experience; recovery with explicit boundaries; measured responsiveness; and eventual authenticated device continuity. The [MVP](MVP.md) deliberately proves only a small slice of these pillars.

Success means a tester can boot, perform a useful typed action, understand and deny its permission, inspect its outcome, and recover an eligible change. Performance and compatibility remain NOT YET MEASURED. Cross-device integration and personal activity memory remain later work.
