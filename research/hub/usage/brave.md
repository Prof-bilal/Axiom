# Brave

## When to use
- You want a Chromium-based browser that blocks ads, trackers, fingerprinting, and cookies by default.
- You want a search engine with its own index that does not build a profile of you (Brave Search).
- You want encrypted sync of bookmarks, passwords, and tabs across your devices.
- You need a private window that routes traffic through Tor for IP masking, with the caveat below.

## How it works
- Shields are the main control: they block ads, trackers, fingerprinting, and cookies, and can be customised per site.
- Brave Search runs on an independent web index.
- Rewards is opt-in: privacy-preserving ads that earn BAT tokens.
- Tor private windows proxy your IP address through Tor; the page does not claim full anonymity.
- A built-in self-custody Wallet and Browser AI (Leo) are also included.

## Not for
- Users who want anonymity guarantees: Brave states that Tor only proxies your IP address.
- Users who do not want crypto or token features; Wallet and Rewards are part of the product, though Rewards is opt-in (Not found: no source says the Wallet can be removed).
