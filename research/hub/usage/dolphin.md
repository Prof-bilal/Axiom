# Dolphin

## When to use
- You want to play GameCube games on a PC with a dedicated emulator.
- You want to play Wii games on a PC.
- You want the emulator's own settings for graphics and controllers for these two consoles.
- You need a Triforce arcade title, which Dolphin also supports.

## How it works
- Dolphin is a standalone emulator built for the GameCube and Wii.
- It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux; on Linux it can be installed from your distribution, from Flathub, or built from source.
- You open the game file in the emulator and play it directly.
- The project's wiki and forums are linked from dolphin-emu.org for setup guidance.

## Not for
- Playing other consoles or handhelds in one frontend; use RetroArch.
- PC games from stores; use Steam, Heroic or Lutris.
