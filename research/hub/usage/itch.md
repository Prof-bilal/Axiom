# itch

## When to use
- You buy or download indie games and tools from itch.io and want them in one desktop app.
- You want the app to download, update and launch your itch.io games for you.
- You want to filter the store for games that are marked as available on Linux.

## How it works
- The itch app is a desktop client for the itch.io store; it is not a replacement for the website.
- Your owned and downloaded games appear in the app's library.
- Downloads, installs, updates and launches are handled by butler, a background component the app uses.
- The app notifies you when an installed game has an update.
- In the store, the Linux tag in the filter sidebar limits results to Linux builds.

## Not for
- Steam, Epic or GOG titles; use Steam or Heroic.
- Running Windows-only games with per-environment Wine control; use Bottles.
