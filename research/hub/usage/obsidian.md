# Obsidian

## When to use
- You want personal notes or a knowledge base stored as plain Markdown files on your own device.
- You want notes that link to each other and a graph view that shows how ideas connect.
- You need notes that work offline and remain readable in open file formats, so you are not locked in.
- You want to extend the app with community plugins and themes.

## How it works
- A vault is a folder of Markdown notes stored on your device.
- Notes link to each other with internal links, and the graph view shows those connections.
- Community plugins and themes customise the app.
- Sync (paid) keeps notes across devices with end-to-end encryption, and Publish (paid) turns notes into a public website.

## Not for
- Real-time team co-editing of documents: the features on the page are personal vault sync and shared files, not a collaborative office suite; use OnlyOffice or LibreOffice instead.
- Free cross-device sync or publishing: those are paid add-ons, and the page does not give prices (Not found).
