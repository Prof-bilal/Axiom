# Krita

## When to use
- You are painting or illustrating with raster brushes and want fine control over brush engines.
- You need comic or concept-art work with layers, masks and filters.
- You want to keep editable, non-destructive layer stacks in a .kra file.
- You need a free, open-source alternative to Photoshop-style painting on Linux.

## How it works
- The main canvas holds a stack of layers that composite from bottom to top.
- Layer types include paint layers (raster artwork), vector layers, group layers and filter layers.
- Masks and filter layers apply edits over the original pixels, so they can be hidden or changed later.
- Brushes are chosen from presets and brush engines; brushes can be loaded and saved.
- Work is saved as a native .kra document that keeps layers and masks intact.

## Not for
- Precise vector logos or print layouts; use Inkscape for vector artwork.
- Photo retouching at the depth of a dedicated photo editor.
