# GCompris

## When to use
- A child aged roughly 2 to 10 needs learning practice on a computer.
- You want early computer skills such as using a keyboard, mouse or touchscreen.
- You want reading, typing, arithmetic, telling time or simple science practice in a playful format.
- You want a free, open-source educational suite with no cost to the family.

## How it works
- GCompris is a suite of more than 100 separate activities, each a small game-style lesson.
- Activities are grouped by topic, including computer discovery, reading, arithmetic, science, logic and music.
- Activities cover letters, words and typing, plus telling time, mazes and musical instruments.
- It is developed by the KDE community and released under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## Not for
- Structured exam or flashcard study for older learners; use Anki.
- Adult productivity or software development; use Git or a document editor.
