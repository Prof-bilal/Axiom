# Bitwarden

## When to use
- You want one end-to-end encrypted vault that syncs logins across browser, desktop, and phone.
- You are a family or small team that wants shared passwords with a low per-user cost.
- You want an open-source password manager that also stores passkeys and secure notes.
- You need a free tier for basic password management, with paid tiers only when you need extras.

## How it works
- Logins, passkeys, and sensitive notes live in one encrypted vault, reachable from any device.
- Autofill works through the browser extension, and apps are listed on the Downloads page.
- Cloud hosting is the default; Enterprise plans add SSO, policies, and self-hosting.
- Premium adds an integrated authenticator, file attachments, and emergency access.

## Not for
- Keeping everything fully offline: the default model is cloud-hosted; choose KeePassXC for a local-only file.
- Self-hosting on a free or personal plan: self-hosting is an Enterprise feature according to the pricing summary.
