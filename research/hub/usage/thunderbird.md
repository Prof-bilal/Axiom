# Thunderbird

## When to use
- You want one desktop app for email, calendar, and contacts instead of a browser-based webmail tab.
- You manage several email accounts and want to read them in a unified inbox or keep them separate.
- You want to keep your mail on your own machine and avoid services that scan inboxes for ads.
- You want to sort and tag messages with filters, and extend the app with add-ons.

## How it works
- Add one or more email accounts; each can be viewed separately or merged into a unified inbox.
- Messages are organised with filters and tags.
- Calendar and contacts sit alongside mail in the same app.
- It is free and open source, funded by donations, and runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Not for
- Chat or task management: the official features page does not mention either (Not found).
- Mobile-only use: the Android version is separate from the desktop app described on the features page.
