# OBS Studio

## When to use
- You want to stream live video to a service such as a video platform.
- You need to record your screen, a window or a camera to a local file.
- You want to switch between layouts, such as a camera scene and a screen scene, during a broadcast.
- You need a free tool that mixes several audio devices for a stream or recording.

## How it works
- Scenes are named layouts that you switch between.
- Sources are items inside a scene, such as windows, displays, cameras, images or text; higher items sit on top.
- The Audio Mixer controls volume for each audio device, with filter options per device.
- The Preview shows where sources are placed; Studio Mode lets you edit a scene privately, then transition it live.
- Streaming sends output to a service with a stream key; recording saves to a local file. Filters and hotkeys add effects and shortcuts.

## Not for
- Editing a recording after capture; use Kdenlive.
- Simply playing media files; use VLC.
