# OnlyOffice

## When to use
- You need an office suite that opens and edits Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files natively (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx).
- You want to edit documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and PDFs offline in one free desktop app.
- You need to fill, create, or sign PDF forms, including adding passwords or digital signatures.
- Your team wants real-time co-editing on its own servers, with the ability to self-host.

## How it works
- Desktop Editors is the free offline app for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- It opens DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX natively, plus ODT, ODS, ODP, and CSV.
- Cloud or server storage (for example Nextcloud or ownCloud) is optional and enables real-time co-editing.
- PDF editing covers text, images, layout, annotations, forms, and conversion to editable documents.

## Not for
- Paid-tier needs: SLAs and priority support require the Enterprise edition, per the official page.
- Users who want no AI features: AI Tools are optional, so they can be left unused, but the page does not describe removing them further (Not found).
