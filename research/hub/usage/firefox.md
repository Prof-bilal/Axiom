# Firefox

## When to use
- You want a privacy-focused browser that blocks trackers and isolates cookies across sites by default.
- You need to keep several accounts for the same site apart using container tabs.
- You prefer a browser run by a non-profit that says it is built to give you control over your data.
- You want to organise many open tabs into groups by project or task.

## How it works
- Tracking protection blocks trackers by default, and total cookie protection stops cookies following you across sites.
- Container tabs keep separate sessions in one window; open a tab in a container from the containers panel, or right-click a link and choose Open link in New Container Tab.
- Tab Groups organise open tabs by project, mood, or task.
- Extensions and themes come from the add-ons site.

## Not for
- Not found: the official feature page does not describe private browsing, profiles, or Sync in detail, so their behaviour is unverified here.
- Containers do not work in private windows, per Mozilla's support material; use a private window for throwaway sessions and containers for separation.
