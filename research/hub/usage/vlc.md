# VLC media player

## When to use
- You need to play a file that other players refuse, such as an unusual codec or container.
- You want to play a DVD, CD or VCD disc directly.
- You want to play network streams or internet radio and podcasts from the playlist.
- You want one player that handles almost any audio or video file.

## How it works
- Open a file by double-clicking it or dragging it onto the window.
- Open a whole folder from the Media menu.
- Open discs by inserting them and choosing Play with VLC when your OS asks.
- The Playlist view lists the current playlist, local media folders, optical drives, local network sources and internet sources such as radio and podcasts.
- Playback controls cover volume, seeking and fullscreen.

## Not for
- Editing or cutting video; use Kdenlive.
- Live capture, streaming or recording your screen; use OBS Studio.
