# Anki

## When to use
- You are memorizing vocabulary, facts, formulas or anything else that needs repeated recall over weeks or months.
- You want review scheduled automatically so cards come back just before you would forget them.
- You are studying for an exam and want a deck of questions you can study daily.
- You want the same cards on desktop and mobile through sync.

## How it works
- A note holds information split into fields, such as a word and its translation.
- Each note creates one card per card type; a card type is a question-and-answer template.
- A note type defines the fields and card types, and is shared across the whole collection.
- Decks group cards for separate study and can be nested with "::"; each deck has its own settings.
- In a review session you see the question, click Show Answer, then rate how well you knew it, which sets when the card returns.

## Not for
- Notes, planning or long-form writing; use a document editor.
- Learning that needs hands-on practice rather than recall; use a course or tutorial app.
