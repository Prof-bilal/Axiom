## Known environment issues

- Old work folders `iso/work-*` and `iso/out-*` hold root-owned files. Remove with `sudo rm -rf`.
- Disk was at 89 to 100 percent once; old ISO build dirs were the cause. Free space before a build.
- A full ISO build takes about 30 minutes. Do not run two builds in the same work dir.
- Mouse input into QEMU from the tools did not work; keyboard does. Use VNC for manual checks.
- Parallel WebSearch batches were rejected by the auto-mode classifier; run one at a time.
- Quickshell window focus: other apps can cover the Hub; use `hyprctl clients -j` to find its geometry.
